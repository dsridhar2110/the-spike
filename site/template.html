The Science behind it all

Data
343,141 London planning applications from UK PlanIt → 149,813 after filters. 18 boroughs, 2018–2025. One table, no joins.
Constraint
Only what is known at submission. Decision date, public comments, who decided, appeal outcome — all excluded. They leak the future.
Features
TF-IDF over 9,343 words and phrases from the application description, plus borough and application type.
Models
Logistic regression (approval) · gradient-boosted quantile regression (timing) · bunching estimator (the spike).
Split
Train 2018–2023 (122,806) · test 2024–2025 (28,073). Time-ordered, never random.
Validation
Walk-forward — retrained and retested 5 times, one year at a time.
Success metric
ROC-AUC 0.7103 · refusal PR-AUC 0.3759 against a 0.1897 base rate · Brier 0.1395.
Stability
0.7085 ± 0.0072 across the five folds — no drift, no lucky year.
Robustness
6 artifact checks on the spike — wrong clock, deadline extensions, one-off period, machine-stamped dates, batch upload, one bad borough. All passed.
